## Why

Listings across many stores produce more reviews and data than anyone can check by hand, and paid tools are built for agencies, not for one brand's way of working.

## What it does

- Fetches listing data from the Google Business Profile API on a schedule
- Analyses listing performance and incoming reviews
- Produces review reports
- Prepares review replies
