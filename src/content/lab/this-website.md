## Why

To learn how far someone who isn't a developer can get by directing AI tools carefully: writing a spec, building in small steps, checking every change and deploying it.

## How it's built

- Designed as a series of prototypes, then built with Astro
- Content lives in Markdown files, so a new log entry is one new file
- Hosted for free, with a custom domain to come
