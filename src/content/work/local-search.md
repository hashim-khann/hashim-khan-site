## Context

The same brand sells through exclusive brand outlets, express stores, retailers and super stockists. Each location can show up on Google Search and Maps.

## The problem

Listings had been created by different people over time. Some were duplicated, some unverified, and access was spread across several accounts. Verifying a store often needed someone physically at the location.

## What I did

- Created and optimised Google Business Profiles across store types
- Ran verification with internal teams, retailers and area managers
- Resolved duplicate listings and access issues
- Tracked a large listing programme across spreadsheets and multiple accounts

## Who did what

- **Me:** the listing workflow, optimisation, tracking and issue resolution
- **Field teams and retailers:** on-site verification and store details
