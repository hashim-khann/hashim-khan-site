## Context

An Indian activewear brand with an online store and a large retail network moved its website from Shopify to a custom-built platform, keeping the same domain.

## The problem

Every URL pattern was changing. Without a complete map from old pages to new ones, pages that ranked would return errors on launch day, and search engines would have to work out the site again from scratch. The new platform also brought new templates, filters and listing pages that needed clear rules for what should and shouldn't be indexed.

## What I did

- Mapped old URLs to new ones and planned the redirects
- Set SEO requirements for the new templates: titles, descriptions, headings, schema, sitemap rules, canonicals and indexing rules
- Reviewed product and category pages for UX and SEO, and turned the findings into change lists for developers
- Planned store pages and supporting content
- Checked page speed before launch
- Ran QA on the staging site, then on launch day, then monitored crawling and indexing afterwards

## Who did what

- **Me:** the SEO plan, URL and redirect mapping, template requirements, audits and QA
- **Developers:** built and deployed the platform
