## Months 1–2: make sure nothing broke

- Check redirects, indexing and crawl errors every week
- Fix template-level issues first, because one fix covers hundreds of pages
- Set a baseline: impressions, clicks, indexed pages and top queries

## Months 3–4: fill the gaps

- Improve category pages with useful copy and internal links
- Build out store and location pages for local search
- Start publishing content that answers real buying questions

## Months 5–6: grow

- Expand content around the topics that are working
- Earn mentions and links from relevant sites
- Review the plan against the baseline and decide what to drop
