## The question

When someone asks an AI assistant what to buy, which brands get named, and which pages does the answer lean on?

## The plan

- Write 15–20 real buying questions, from broad ("best gym t-shirts") to specific ("quick-dry t-shirt for running in humid weather")
- Ask each assistant the same questions on the same day
- Record which brands are mentioned and which sources are cited
- Look for patterns: guides vs product pages, marketplaces vs brand sites, reviews vs specs

## What I expect

Buying guides and comparison pages will be cited far more than product pages. If that holds, it changes what an e-commerce brand should publish.
